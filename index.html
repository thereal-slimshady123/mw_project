ABOUT THIS STUDY

Welcome to BREATHEBEAT

We are comparing two kinds of short breaks to see how well each one helps your mind recover after a stressful task. The whole session takes about 15 minutes.

What you will do

  1. A short warm-up color task.
  2. Part 1 and Part 2. Each part has the same steps:
    • 45 seconds of quick arithmetic (meant to be a bit stressful)
    • A short color task and two quick questions
    • A 3-minute break with your eyes closed
    • The color task again and a few quick questions
  3. Two final questions comparing the breaks.

The two breaks (in random order)

Silent Break

Close your eyes and sit in silence. Breathe slowly at your own pace.

Audio Break

Close your eyes and breathe in sync with the audio. Inhale on the short sound (4 s), exhale on the long sound (6 s).

Before you start

  • Sit somewhere quiet and use headphones or earphones if you have them.
  • Put your phone away for the whole session.
  • There are no right or wrong answers. Answer honestly.
